What We're Built On

One center. Three expressions.

We're not organized around a personality or a program. Every ministry here exists to point back to one thing.

The Word

Scripture taught in full — not fragments picked to fit a mood. You'll leave knowing what the Bible actually says, and why it matters this week.

Worship

Unhurried, honest worship that makes room for God to move — healing, direction, and presence, not just a set list.

Community

Small groups and family gatherings where people actually know your name and carry your week with you.

Mission

What's taught here is meant to leave the building — in how we work, give, and love our city and beyond.

This Sunday

You'll be welcome the moment you walk in.

No membership required to attend, no dress code, no pressure. Just come, and let's take it from there. Kids' church runs at both services.
